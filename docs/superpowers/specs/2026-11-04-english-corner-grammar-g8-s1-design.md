# English corner: Grade 8 Semester 1 grammar — design

**Status:** proposed (this PR; nothing is built yet). **Scope:** grammar activities (Choose, Build, True or false) for the ten
Grade 8 Semester 1 lessons whose book statement is a grammar topic: nine already in the English hub, and one (`u5_l4`) that
becomes a **grammar-only hub lesson**. Builds on the authored-items engine (`lib/curriculum/src/englishGrammar.ts`), the
grammar-only hub mechanism (`GRAMMAR_ONLY_LESSONS`, `hubLessonVisible`) and the generalised teacher form
(`englishGrammarReview.ts`) of the Grade 9 and Grade 10 work (`2026-10-23-english-corner-grammar-g10-design.md`,
`2026-10-24-english-corner-grammar-g10-s2-design.md`). Not in this spec: Semester 2's grammar lessons (a separate spec and PR),
audio, signing off any unit, the sentence-level vocabulary check, Grades 6 and 7.

## Why

Grades 1–5, 9 and 10 have grammar activities; Grades 6, 7 and 8 have none. Grade 8's hub already carries 57 lessons from the
Word List pass (both semesters), but no grammar. Each lesson's `objectives[0]` in
`lib/curriculum/src/data/iqra_curriculum_g8_eng_sem1.json` is an «I can» statement, as in Grades 9 and 10, so the statement
names the grammar point and a teacher can check it against the printed book page. Ten of Semester 1's 35 lessons are grammar
(the others are vocabulary, reading, speaking, listening and writing lessons, which this work does not touch). Grade 8 uses the
same data and engine as Grades 9 and 10, so no new infrastructure is needed. Grades 6 and 7 would first need their book text
read in (Grade 6's catalog has no per-lesson statements; Grade 7 is not in the hub), which is a larger job.

## Decisions taken (and why)

1. **Semester 1 only, 10 lessons** (chosen in conversation, 2026-11-04). Alternatives offered: both semesters at once (one larger
   PR, about 800 items for a teacher to check in one go), and Grade 6 or 7 instead (needs the book text first). Semester 2 follows
   as its own spec, as Grade 10 did.
2. **Hand-written items, 40 a lesson, 400 in all**, as Grades 9 and 10: whole sentences of nine words or fewer, a correct answer
   and a distractor, no names, no pictures, no Yes or No, True or false silent, every unit `reviewedBy: null`.
3. **Mostly `form` distractors** (chosen in conversation). Grade 8 pupils are younger than Grade 10's, and several lessons
   (Present Simple, there is/are, quantifiers, Past Simple) are about getting the form right. A distractor is `form`
   (ungrammatical in standard English: the wrong agreement, a missing auxiliary, `-s` or `-ed` in the wrong place, `much` with a
   countable noun, a misspelt `-ing` or `-ed` form) or `meaning` (grammatical, but ruled out by a word in the sentence itself:
   `yesterday`, `every day`, `now`, `Look!`, or a «so» / «but» clause for can / can't). `meaning` is used only in the four
   sub-topics pinned below: 52 `meaning` items and 348 `form` items. Never a distractor that real English accepts.
4. **`u5_l4` is a grammar-only hub lesson** (listed in `GRAMMAR_ONLY_LESSONS`): it has no vocabulary rows, so it is invisible
   until a teacher signs the unit, shows grammar cards only, and carries the index row «grammar activities», as Articles and the
   Grade 9 and 10 grammar-only lessons do. Grade 8 goes from 57 to 58 hub lessons.
5. **Grade 8 gets its own printed teacher form and sheet** (`docs/english-grammar-g8-review.html`, `-sheet.md`), generated by the
   existing renderers; Grade 8 is added to the generator's grade list and `GRADE_COPY` gains a Grade 8 entry.
6. **Overlap with Grades 9 and 10 is accepted.** Grade 10 Semester 1 also teaches the Present Simple and Continuous; the Grade 8
   items are written separately, with simpler sentences and mostly `form` choices.
7. **Every unit ships `reviewedBy: null`**; nothing appears to a pupil until a teacher signs the printed form.
8. **No «ما الجديد» entry in this PR.** Nothing is visible to a user until a unit is signed (CLAUDE.md asks for an entry when a
   change is user-visible); the entry comes with the PR that sets the first `reviewedBy`.

Sizes sum to 40 a lesson (400 in all) and are pinned by a test; that makes 52 `meaning` items (`can-vs-cant` 14, `habit-markers`
12, `now-markers` 12, `past-time-markers` 14) and 348 `form` items. The `statement` column is the book's `objectives[0]`
shortened; the data file carries it verbatim and a test reads it back. Examples are illustrative; the written items are the
authority and are listed on the form. As in Grades 9 and 10, a `meaning` sub-topic may switch to `form` during the build when a
distractor turns out to fit; the pinned sizes, the totals and the status log then say so.

Rules for every item:

1. Nine words or fewer; one grammar point; what decides the answer is in the sentence.
2. No real person's name (a pronoun, a family word such as `Mum` or `Dad`, or a Jordanian place); nothing religious, political or
   sensitive; school-level words, with few outside the Grade 8 lists (they print on the form for the teacher).
3. A `form` distractor is ungrammatical in standard English. Spelling distractors (`runing`, `studyed`) are allowed only where the
   book teaches the spelling rule (`spelling-es`, `spelling-ing`, `regular-ed`).
4. A `meaning` distractor is used only where a marker in the sentence rules it out: `yesterday`, `last week`, `in 2020` for the
   Past Simple against a present form; `every day`, `usually`, `on Fridays` and `now`, `Look!`, `Listen!`, `at the moment` for
   Simple against Continuous (never `this week` or `always`, where both fit); and a «so» or «but» clause that states the clue for
   can / can't («He is three, so he can't wash dishes.»). A clause both modals could follow is not enough.
5. An adverb sits inside the answer so the false twin keeps standard word order (`is always` / `always is`).
6. An uncountable-noun item uses only nouns whose plural is not standard at this level (`milk`, `rice`, `bread`, `meat`, `butter`,
   `cheese`, `water`), never `sugar` or `fruit`, which have a real plural.
7. A question item whose distractor is a grammatical question in another word order (`Is there a sofa?` against `Is a sofa there?`)
   is not written; the twin must be ungrammatical.

## Where it lives

- **`lib/curriculum/src/englishGrammarG8Items.ts`** (new): `G8_ITEM_LESSONS`, ten entries, in the shape of `englishGrammarG10Items.ts`
  (`{ lessonId, statement, items }`, items built with the same `items(topic, kind, rows)` helper); imports only types from the engine.
- **`lib/curriculum/src/englishGrammarG8.ts`** (new): `GRAMMAR_UNITS_G8` (one unit per lesson, `reviewedBy: null`, sorted into hub order
  from `hubLessonsForGrade(8)`) and `NOT_COVERED_G8`, one list of notes per lesson printed on the form (what the lesson leaves out and
  why; no `"`, `&`, `<`, `>`, no glued «و»), as `englishGrammarG10.ts`.
- **`lib/curriculum/src/englishGrammar.ts`:** import `GRAMMAR_UNITS_G8` and add it to `GRAMMAR_UNITS`. The engine, `unitSentences`,
  `offeredGrammar` and the sheet renderer need no change (Grade 8 has no picture, so the picture-free sheet layout of Grades 5, 9
  and 10 applies).
- **`lib/curriculum/src/englishHub.ts`:** `GRAMMAR_ONLY_LESSONS` gains `kbl-g8-eng-s1-nccd-u5_l4`; the doc comment is updated. The
  unit title for the lesson's hub entry comes from the existing Grade 8 catalog (`catalogs/g8EngSem1.ts`); the plan confirms the
  catalog carries the lesson and its unit before building.
- **`lib/curriculum/src/englishGrammarReview.ts`:** `GRADE_COPY[8]` (title, eyebrow, lead naming the ten lessons and Semester 1, the
  activities text, the checks) in the style of Grades 9 and 10; the form has one section per unit, so ten pages, each labelled with
  its semester and unit title by the existing code.
- **`lib/curriculum/scripts/grammar-sheet.ts`:** `8` is added to the grade list; the Grade 8 sheet and form are generated and committed.
- **Docs:** the write-up at the top of `docs/status-log.md` and an index line in `STATUS.md`.

No engine, app, server or schema change. Progress stays on the device, keyed by lesson id.

## Tests

- **Data (`englishGrammarG8Items.test.ts`, new):** ten lessons, 400 items, every sub-topic size and kind pinned; each statement
  equals `objectives[0]` of its lesson id in the Grade 8 Semester 1 JSON; every sentence nine words or fewer, unique, the answer
  once, a differing twin that is never another true sentence; kinds only `form` or `meaning`, `meaning` only in the four pinned
  sub-topics; no capitalised name outside a whitelist; the glued-«و» check passes over the Grade 8 form; the committed Grade 8
  sheet and form equal the generator.
- **Units (`englishGrammarG8.test.ts`, new):** ten units in hub order, all `reviewedBy: null`, none offered, a not-covered note each.
- **Hub (`englishHub.test.ts`, `englishHubVisible.test.ts`):** `GRAMMAR_ONLY_LESSONS` has one more id and the existing «no words and
  a grammar unit» check covers it; Grade 8 has 58 lessons; the existing Grade 8 test, which compares the hub with the lessons that
  reach `MIN_HUB_WORDS` words, is changed to add the grammar-only lesson (it fails otherwise); the other grades' counts are unchanged.
- **App (`artifacts/mobile/services/__tests__/englishGrammarG8ItemGames.test.ts`, new):** Choose, Build and True or false are offered
  on all ten lessons, Yes or No on none, True or false silent, rounds non-empty.
- **CI's own list:** `pnpm run typecheck`, curriculum `test` and `verify`, mobile `pnpm test`, api-server build and test.

The screens are checked in a browser at 390 px in Arabic and English with temporary local sign-offs restored afterwards: a Grade 8
lesson without a signed unit shows no grammar row; `u5_l4` is absent from the Grade 8 index and its URL says «lesson not found»
until signed; signed, it appears with only Choose, Build and True or false; no horizontal scroll.

## Open points for the teacher (printed on the form)

1. Does each lesson id match the printed book page and its grammar line?
2. Is any distractor ever acceptable in your classroom, and is every `meaning` item really decided by its marker?
3. Are the sentences clear and at the right level for Grade 8, and is the vocabulary right?
4. Is the spelling distractor (`runing`, `studyed`) a fair thing to test, or should spelling be left out?

## Known limits

- The 400 sentences are ours; a teacher checks each correct answer and distractor on the printed form and may change some. A
  signed unit would need those fixed first. The four `meaning` sub-topics are judgment calls.
- Grade 8's Semester 1 has 25 other lessons (vocabulary, reading, speaking, listening, writing) that get no grammar activity here.
- The vocabulary check is a printed list, not a test; no audio is recorded (`english-audio` needs the OpenAI and R2 keys); True or
  false is silent by design.
- A grammar-only lesson that appears only after a signature is behaviour tested but not yet seen on a device.

## Switching a lesson on

A Grade 8 English teacher signs that lesson's page of `docs/english-grammar-g8-review.html`, printed, and checks the book-page line;
audio is recorded for the correct sentences; then the unit's `reviewedBy` is set in `englishGrammarG8.ts` (with a «ما الجديد» entry).
The lesson appears in the hub from that moment.

## Not done here

- Signing off any unit: that is the teacher's.
- Grade 8 Semester 2, Grade 6 and Grade 7.
