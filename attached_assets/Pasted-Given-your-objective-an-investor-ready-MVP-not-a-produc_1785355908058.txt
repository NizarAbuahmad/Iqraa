# Demo Story

By the end of the MVP, your investor should experience this journey:

1. Open IQRA.
2. Select **Grade 10 → Mathematics → Unit → Lesson**.
3. Ask IQRA a curriculum-aware question.
4. Generate a complete lesson plan, worksheet, quiz, and classroom activity.
5. Tap **Start Lesson** to launch an interactive classroom with presentation mode, teacher notes, and timers.
6. See everything automatically organized in the Teacher Workspace.
7. Finish by showing vision screens for Students, Parents, and Schools, illustrating how IQRA evolves into a complete AI-powered education platform.

## My Recommendation

I would **remove Task #6 (Real AI)** from your immediate roadmap and move it to **Phase 3**.

For an investor demo, **mock AI is sufficient** if the outputs are realistic and the user experience is polished. Investors are evaluating the product vision, user workflow, and differentiation—not whether the responses are generated live. The features that will have the biggest impact on your pitch are the **Interactive Classroom**, **Lesson Flow Engine**, **Teacher Workspace**, and a seamless, polished demo experience. Those are what will make IQRA stand out from generic AI tools.
